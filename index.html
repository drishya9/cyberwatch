How to use CyberWatch Quick start +

You need an internet connection for lookups. Your watchlist stays in this browser; no sign-in is needed.

  1. Search a vulnerability

    Enter a CVE identifier such as CVE-2021-44228 and select Analyze CVE, or press Enter. You can also choose one of the example buttons. Wait for the lookup to finish.

  2. Read the evidence

    EPSS probability estimates exploitation likelihood over the next 30 days. Percentile is the score’s relative rank, not another probability. Known exploited means CISA lists this CVE. Check the score and catalog dates.

  3. Understand the recommendation

    High, Medium, and Lower are CyberWatch’s custom review categories. Open Behind the recommendation for the thresholds. Missing sources are marked unavailable; a low score or “Not listed” does not establish safety.

  4. Save and revisit

    Select Save to watchlist after a lookup. Refreshing keeps saved IDs, not the result cards. Choose Recheck to load an ID again, or Remove to delete it from your list. Duplicate saves are prevented.

  5. Export your watchlist

    Select Export CSV to create a file containing your saved IDs. A copyable preview also appears. If your browser blocks the automatic download, use Download CSV or copy the text. Open the CSV in a spreadsheet or text editor.

  6. Handle a missing result

    A format warning means you should check the ID. No EPSS record means the ID has no returned score; it may lack coverage or may not exist. Partial results mean one source failed. Check your connection and retry; requests stop waiting after 15 seconds.

About saved data

The watchlist belongs to this browser and page address. Clearing browser storage, using private browsing, or switching browsers or preview addresses can give you a different list. If storage is blocked, the app explains that changes will last only until refresh.

Behind the recommendation Understand the rules +
01 · Collect

Fetch public JSON from FIRST EPSS and CISA’s official KEV mirror. Each source is handled independently.

02 · Evaluate

High: CISA-listed or EPSS ≥ 10%. Medium: not listed and EPSS ≥ 1%. Lower: not listed and EPSS < 1%. Missing evidence is labeled incomplete or insufficient.

03 · Remember

Store CVE IDs with localStorage. No account or server database is needed. Scores are retrieved again when you recheck.

These are custom student-project rules, not official severity ratings. A low score or absence from KEV does not establish safety. EPSS estimates probability, not impact.
